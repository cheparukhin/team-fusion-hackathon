INTERPRETATION FIRST

Every connection needs its own evidence.

Our target is membership in the published NanoString-supported list among mapped probe-panel pairs. A pair absent from that list is not reported supported; it is not a proven false chimera or a confirmed assay failure.

Probe design, confirmed testing, QC, exact junctions, and read provenance remain separate. Unknown assay status stays unknown. Pair-level labels never establish which isoform was supported.

The explorer shows the 479 eligible pairs. The original 529 probe designs map to 527 distinct ordered pairs; 48 unresolved candidate/control pairs are excluded from the model. Sequence-backed identity reconciliation is documented in the dataset audit.
