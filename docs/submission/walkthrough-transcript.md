## Slide 2

There are three different questions: is a gene pair supported at the RNA level, what exact junction joins it, and is a protein translated and functional? We reconstructed 479 eligible ordered pairs, including 109 reported NanoString-supported pairs. Those 109 are not 109 verified structures. Unknown support is also not a proven biological negative. Our workflow keeps these layers distinct.

## Slide 4

For Psap–Lgals3, six published read identifiers support a candidate gene pair. When we compare the published endpoints with the sequence-mapped probe, the nearest second-parent endpoint differs by 924 genomic bases. This schematic marks the two positions on chromosome 14. We independently reproduced the coordinate calculation from supplementary tables and reference transcripts.
The discrepancy does not establish that the probe is wrong or the RNA is false. The original alignment and coordinate convention still need reconciliation. It changes the immediate question: inspect the source alignment before choosing a junction-specific assay. Our two-million-pair short-read subset does not match this probe junction under the fixed rule; bounded non-detection is not proof of absence.

## Slide 5

Gsdmd–Tmem106a illustrates another trap. The paper reports protein and functional experiments. Our cached 118 amino acid reference-sequence hypothesis produces a low-confidence Boltz2 model, with mean p L D D T 48.7. Its red and blue segments denote parent contributions, not confidence. The picture is not an experimentally solved fusion structure. A weak computational model cannot erase published experimental evidence. We preserve both facts and specify the remaining question for a new sample.

## Slide 6

We also tested whether adding Hi-C improves prioritisation. On the same 401 complete-evidence pairs, held-out average precision was 0.296 for RNA features and 0.300 with Hi-C. The bars use a zero-to-one scale. The difference is small and the paired gene-component bootstrap interval spans zero. Five-fold evaluation separates shared parent genes across folds. We therefore do not claim an established ranking gain. These retrospective labels measure reported NanoString support, not absolute RNA authenticity.
