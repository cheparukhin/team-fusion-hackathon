# Decisions established during execution

The original scope remains a minimal evidence-prioritization experiment. These findings refine its implementation:

1. **Panel:** 529 probe designs map by exact sequence evidence to 527 ordered pairs. Five `_2` design suffixes are resolved against GENCODE M28. Two pairs have repeated designs. The 48 pairs absent from every supplied long-read catalogue/caller list have unresolved biological-candidate/control status and are excluded. Final evaluation: 479 pairs, 109 reported NanoString positives.
2. **Missing evidence:** Public tables do not map long-read IDs to biological samples. Sample recurrence remains missing and contributes no predictive signal. Unreported NanoString support is never treated as a confirmed failed assay or biological negative.
3. **Hi-C:** Three public control-siRNA/LPS processed replicates contain 500-kb KR-balanced data. Features use the paper's GENOVA local enrichment definition, averaged over distinct probe bin pairs and then independently normalized replicates. This differs from the paper's pooled-replicate processing and is documented. All three replicates are required for the primary feature.
4. **Normalization correction:** Independent review found that the native reader omits invalid normalized records. Explicit KR-vector masking now distinguishes unavailable pixels from measured zero contacts. This invalidated 41 formerly usable windows across 14 panel pairs; final complete-contact cohort: 401 pairs, 92 positives.
5. **Matched training:** Both RNA and RNA+Hi-C models are trained and evaluated on identical complete-contact rows within the fixed gene-disjoint folds. This prevents contact availability from masquerading as contact-enrichment benefit. A separate RNA model uses the full 479-pair panel for missing-Hi-C fallback.
6. **Result:** Full-panel RNA AP is 0.296 versus 0.268 for read support. In the matched spatial comparison, RNA AP is 0.296 and RNA+Hi-C AP is 0.300; the difference 0.00364 has a 95% component-bootstrap interval of −0.0310 to 0.0469. Improvement is not established; matched top-20 recovery is 10 versus 9. No tuning was performed to improve the result.
7. **Exploratory application:** The refit ranks 29,911 already published candidates outside the probe panel. These are unvalidated out-of-panel scores, not newly established chRNAs. Feature extrapolation is flagged.
8. **OpenAI:** Five source-grounded reports were actually authored by the Codex agent, with source snapshots, input hashes, and review. The live Responses API adapter is implemented; no API call occurred because no key was available. Cached authored reports and deterministic fallback summaries are distinguished in the UI.
9. **Compute:** A new bounded Brev A100 pilot was authorized from a live $1.80/hour quote, with a two-hour lifecycle cap. Its actual execution status, outputs, estimated cost, and termination are recorded separately in results/compute/pilot_summary.json and spending_manifest.json. No existing user instance was stopped or deleted.

The final independent review and exact verification hashes are in results/dataset_reconstruction/independent_review.md and final_review_verification.json. The five-minute presentation runbook is results/demo/presentation.md.
